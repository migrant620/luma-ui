IMAGES
------------------------------------------------------------

All listing photographs, host/room imagery and the map illustration are
original works produced for this prototype:

- Listing photos (h01..h06.webp) and photo-tour images (t01..t06.webp):
  generated with an image-generation model (ImageGen) from original text
  prompts. They are NOT screenshots, recolourings or derivatives of any
  Airbnb, host or third-party photograph, and carry no third-party
  copyright. They are distributed under the prototype's noncommercial
  study licence. See apps/airbnb/spec/content-manifest.json for the
  per-image record.

- Map background (Results screen): drawn from primitives (roads, a water
  body, blocks) in react-native-svg. It is a stylised illustration, not a
  real geographic map tile, so no map-tile copyright applies.

No Airbnb brand graphics, logos, icons or avatar illustrations are used.
